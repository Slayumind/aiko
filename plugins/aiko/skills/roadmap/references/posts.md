# Posts and errands around the roadmap

## A post to the group's channel

Written in the user's name, sent by the user. The usual shape after an update:

1. **Thanks with a reason.** Name what the person sent and what it gave: "your pages became the
   model for my domains".
2. **What changed, with the link.** Two or three lines: what is new on the page and where.
3. **The owner's statement.** "I proofread my domains: they match how I see the tasks, why they
   matter and their order." The owner answers for their own part only.
4. **The ask, per person.** The exact missing items for each named person ("domain borders, who is
   responsible, where to read statuses"), and what it unlocks for them ("then you can proofread
   your part").

Before showing the draft:
- If a phrase of the user's request reads two ways, say how you read it.
- If someone not named is in the same position (also missing the same items), ask whether to add
  them; do not add them silently.
- Run the writing check. "Ты" to one person and "вы" to two others in one post is fine.
- Names of people stay as text in the draft; remind the user to pick them from the chat's mention
  list when pasting.

## When a colleague's answer opens a class of problems

An answer that explains one case often shows a pattern ("31 cards have the wrong branch").

1. **Match it to the queue.** Look for asks of the same kind already there; one cause is cheaper to
   find once than three times.
2. **Split the fix.** A quick manual fix of the known cases now, and a separate ask to find where
   the mismatches come from. The manual fix may touch other systems: ask the owner of that data what
   else changes before anyone edits.
3. **Ask about the zone before filing.** By the process, the domain owner files an ask and a
   requester accepts the result. Ask the colleague whether they will be the customer or whether the
   topic belongs to another domain, and show the user the question first.
4. **Personal data stays where it is.** A sheet with students' or clients' data is linked, never
   copied into the roadmap. The roadmap carries the link and the numbers.
